Front garden perspective render showing cantilevered portico, timber louver accents, and expansive glazed fenestrations.

The Architectural Challenge

The client envisioned an open-concept contemporary villa that seamlessly blurred the boundary between landscaped outdoor patios and indoor living lounges, while maintaining thermal and structural integrity:

  • Structural Cantilevers: Calculating balanced cantilevered canopies and shading overhangs that provide passive cooling without requiring bulky column supports.
  • Glazing & Material Harmony: Coordinating large floor-to-ceiling glass assemblies with warm natural timber cladding, smooth white plaster, and charcoal metal trims.
  • Construction-Ready BIM Base: Developing a 3D model that serves not just for marketing renders, but provides exact wall thicknesses, structural beam locations, and construction drawings.

The Ribhus Engineering Approach

  1. ArchiCAD Massing & Sunlight Study: Evaluated seasonal sun angles to position shading louvers and optimize natural winter heat gain while blocking summer glare.
  2. Parametric Joinery & Assemblies: Configured multi-layered wall assemblies with thermal insulation cores and waterproof flashing around large sliding glass apertures.
  3. Photorealistic Rendering in Twinmotion: Applied physically-based rendering (PBR) materials, realistic lighting reflections, and contextual landscape foliage.
  4. Comprehensive Elevation Sheets: Extracted dimensioned exterior elevations and boundary section cuts directly from the BIM database.
