The Engineering Challenge

Multi-storey residential modeling demands flawless vertical coordination. In this G+3 complex, the client needed an integrated ArchiCAD BIM model that eliminated discrepancies between architectural design intent and structural load-bearing requirements:

  • Shaft & Core Alignment: Maintaining exact vertical continuity of plumbing ducts, rainwater downpipes, and staircase headroom across all 4 levels.
  • Floor-by-Floor Variability: Ground floor incorporated entrance lobbies and utility parking, while upper floors hosted typical residential apartment layouts.
  • Unified Elevation & Section Extraction: Producing consistent 2D technical drawings directly linked to the central 3D model, ensuring any future design changes propagate across all drawing sheets automatically.

The Ribhus Approach & Technical Execution

Our civil engineering team structured the project using modular ArchiCAD storey settings, hotlinked modules, and parametric composite assemblies:

  1. Datum & Grid Definition: Locked column grid lines and storey-to-storey elevations to prevent level drift across floors.
  2. Hotlinked Floor Modules: Standardized repetitive apartment units as modular hotlinks to ensure rapid updates and file size optimization.
  3. Staircase Core & Headroom Clearance: Modeled flight stairs with code-mandated handrails, landings, and 2.1m minimum head clearances.
  4. Façade Modulation: Integrated recessed balconies, shading louvers, and composite exterior insulation systems (ETICS/WDVS).
  5. IFC Classification & Property Sets: Categorized every wall, slab, beam, and opening in accordance with ISO 19650 standards.
