The Design & Engineering Challenge

Designing a spacious 3-bedroom residence on a 32 x 50 ft plot requires solving tight spatial constraints without sacrificing natural light, ventilation, or privacy:

  • Zoning Separation: Clear functional demarcation between formal guest entertaining zones, semi-private family dining/living spaces, and completely secluded private bedrooms.
  • Ventilation in Compact Footprints: Ensuring both master suites and communal rooms benefit from natural cross-ventilation and daylight penetration despite common boundary walls.
  • Integrated 2D-to-BIM Workflow: Producing fully dimensioned architectural working drawings, structural wall sections, and door/window schedules that remain live-linked to the 3D model.

The Ribhus Engineering Solution

Our civil engineers crafted an optimized layout utilizing central light shafts and open-plan circulation:

  1. Optimized Floor Zoning: Placed formal drawing room at the entrance, transitioning smoothly into a central double-height dining courtyard.
  2. Daylight Lightwell & Courtyard: Integrated an internal green courtyard to channel sunlight and passive cool air through the building core.
  3. Parametric Wall & Slab Assemblies: Defined 9-inch load-bearing peripheral walls and 4.5-inch acoustic partition walls in ArchiCAD.
  4. Linked BIMx Interactive Presentation: Generated an interactive mobile model allowing client and contractors to walk through the layout on iPad/smartphones with live dimension measurement.
