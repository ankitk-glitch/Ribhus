Why Energy Consultancies Outsource Modeling

An energy audit is only as good as the model under it.

Auditors and consultants receive plans as PDFs, DWGs or scans. Before any calculation can start, someone has to rebuild them as a model with correct wall thicknesses, floor heights, openings and room volumes. Hiring locally is slow and costly, and generic CAD outsourcing often returns models that fail your own checks. Ribhus is a small civil engineering studio that does only this one job, and does it to a standard you can build on.

Clear Answers

Questions we hear before the first project.

Do you do the energy calculation too?

No. We build the model your calculation starts from. The calculation and any certificate stay with your team.

What can I send you?

PDFs, DWG or DXF files, scans and photos of hand-drawn plans. Clearer sources give faster results, and we flag unclear dimensions before we model them.

Which software and formats do you deliver?

ArchiCAD (.PLN), Revit (.RVT), IFC 4.3, and DWG or PDF drawings. BIMx is available for client review.

What level of detail do you work at?

LOD 200 and LOD 300. If you need something else, say so when you ask for the quote.

How fast is it really?

Standard residential models take 24–48 hours from the moment we have complete drawings. Larger projects get a timeline in the quote.

How do you make sure the model is right?

Every model is checked for dimensions, wall thicknesses, floor heights, standards and a clean IFC export before it reaches you.

Is my data safe?

We sign an NDA on request, use your files only for your project, and delete them when you ask. Our privacy policy explains how we handle personal data.

What does the free pilot include?

One room, one floor plan or one scanned PDF, modelled free. No card, no contract. It lets you judge accuracy and speed before committing.
