Why us?

We are a specialized, boutique architectural BIM conversion studio.

Founded by Ankit Kumar and Madhav Bhati — both Civil Engineers from Delhi Technological University (DTU) — Ribhus was built to solve a critical bottleneck: European architectural firms and energy consultants need fast, dimensionally accurate BIM conversions without the prohibitive cost or lag of local staffing.

We are not a generic offshore CAD factory. We are a specialized civil engineering studio that understands European building codes, DIN standards, and energy model requirements inside out.
